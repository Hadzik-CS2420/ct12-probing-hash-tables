3. search — why DELETED is not EMPTY

  
Eve hashes to 5, but she is sitting in 6 because slot 5 was taken when she arrived.
     Diana has since been removed, so slot 5 is now a tombstone. What the search does at that slot
     decides whether Eve is ever found again.

4. insert — the tombstone trap

  
Re-inserting Eve, whose slot 5 is now a tombstone and who is actually living in slot 6.
     Dropping her into the first tombstone the moment you see it is the single most common bug in this
     file, and it does not look like a bug — it looks like a table with two Eves.

5. resize — tombstones swept away

  
Growing from 7 slots to 17. Only OCCUPIED entries are re-inserted, so the
     tombstone is never carried across — a resize is the only thing that cleans them up. Every key
     gets a new index, because the index came from % capacity_.
